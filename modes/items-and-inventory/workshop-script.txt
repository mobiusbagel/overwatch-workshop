## How to export from the game

1. Open your custom game lobby.
2. Settings → Workshop.
3. Click **Copy Settings** (copies the entire script as text).
4. Paste the clipboard contents into this file, replacing everything above.

## How to import

1. Copy the entire contents of this file.
2. In a custom game lobby: Settings → Workshop → **Import Settings**, paste.
   (The Import button appears once text is on the clipboard.)
