## How to export from the game

1. Open your Skirmish placer lobby.
2. Settings → Workshop.
3. Click **Copy Settings**, paste the clipboard contents into this file,
   replacing everything above.

## How to import

1. Copy the entire contents of this file.
2. In a custom game lobby: Settings → Workshop → **Import Settings**, paste.
